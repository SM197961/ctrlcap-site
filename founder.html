The seat I came from

More than fifteen years in private real estate lending operations: loan servicing, fund control, asset management, workout, and secondary-market loan sales, across RTL, fix-and-flip, and DSCR product. At Nvestor Funding I joined the founding team as SVP and was there from two people to roughly $300 million in assets under management. Growth like that teaches you something specific: the process that works at ten loans a month quietly stops working at a hundred, and nobody sends a notice when it happens.

Why I build

For most of my career, the answer to a broken process was another spreadsheet or a vendor quote due back in two quarters. I got tired of knowing exactly what the system should do and not being able to make it exist, so I learned to build. An MBA at the W. P. Carey School of Business followed, with a concentration in artificial intelligence, and the working method now is AI-native: design the system, then build it with AI as a collaborator rather than wait on someone else's roadmap. That is the reason a fixed-scope 90-day build is a real offer instead of an optimistic one.

I am not a consultant who learned lending. I am a lending operator who learned to ship software.
